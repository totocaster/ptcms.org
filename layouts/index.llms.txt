# Plaintext Commons

> {{ site.Params.description }} Every page on this site is also plain Markdown: add index.md to its address.

The manifesto sets out the stance: a folder of plain text files you control is the system of record, and tools assist without owning it. The formats are versioned specifications. Each version lives at a frozen address and its rules have stable IDs (such as T-10) that can be cited.
