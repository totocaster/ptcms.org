Plaintext Commons is a design stance for knowledge that should outlast software: a folder of plain text files you control is the shared, durable substrate for people, tools and agents. This site holds the [manifesto](/manifesto/) and the [formats](/formats/) that follow from it.

## How the site is built {#how}

The site is static. Every page is also available as plain Markdown at `index.md` beside it, so you can read it in a terminal, feed it to an agent, or keep a copy in your own corpus. The [formats catalogue](/formats.json) and each version's `rules.json` describe the specifications for programs.

Specifications are versioned and frozen: once a version is published at its address, its text never changes. The rules for addresses and versions are on [How formats mature](/formats/process/#section-2).

## Reuse {#reuse}

The manifesto is released into the public domain. Its source, with its history, is in the [Plaintext Commons repository](https://github.com/totocaster/plaintext-commons).

The initial version of the manifesto is written by [Toto Tvalavadze](https://ttvl.co).
