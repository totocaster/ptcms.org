title: "Plaintext Commons"
description: "Open, versioned plain-text formats and the manifesto behind them. Knowledge should outlast software."
# Quoted from the manifesto. The texts around them, and the three figures, are in data/home.yaml.
claim: "A single folder of plain text files you control can act as the shared, durable substrate for people, tools, and agents—a neutral commons where no app owns the data and any tool may participate."
problem: "Most modern software is cloud-first and app-centric. Each product keeps your data in its own format and database, only reluctantly offering exports and brittle imports."
consequence: "Store what matters in plain text (Markdown or `.txt`) inside a corpus you own. Let tools come and go; the folder remains the source of truth and the bridge between them."
laws:
  - title: "The folder is the platform."
    text: "One corpus—a folder of open files—acts as the system of record."
  - title: "The file is the protocol."
    text: "Stable, human-readable plain text (Markdown or `.txt`) with optional frontmatter. No opaque blobs."
  - title: "Writes are previewed and reviewable."
    text: "Tools propose changes; you preview and accept; a simple log/history preserves why changes happened, not just what changed."
